# Verification

## Automated checks

From the project directory, run:

```sh
npm test
npm run check
```

The focused suite covers configuration isolation, current-site overrides, exact and wildcard rule precedence, independent Any Copy runtimes, No Autoplay audio permission, direct media-play intent, image grouping and original-source ranking, Bilibili calendar-day handling, locale selection, page runtimes, Manifest V3 permissions, local-only assets, script syntax, and interface constraints.

## Chrome checks

1. Open `chrome://extensions`, enable Developer mode, and load the `extension` folder as an unpacked extension.
2. Open the popup and confirm that it contains two rows. The first contains Native Scroll, Native Scroll Enhanced, No Autoplay, and No Autoplay Enhanced. The second contains Any Copy, Any Copy Enhanced, Image Download, and Video Download. Confirm that All Settings is beside the fixed Cosmic Gemini wordmark.
3. Confirm that inactive primary products use a stronger neutral color than Enhanced controls. Active Native Scroll, No Autoplay, Any Copy, and Any Copy Enhanced controls must be blue with a background. Active Image Download and Video Download controls must be blue without a background. No popup product state should use green.
4. Turn the Native Scroll global default off in Settings, then enable it from the popup on one website. Confirm that it remains active only through that exact-site override. Turn the global default on, disable the same website from the popup, and confirm that the exact-site override keeps it inactive. Repeat with No Autoplay.
5. Add `*.example.com` to an Enhanced list, then turn Enhanced off on a matching exact hostname. Confirm that the website remains active in Standard mode. Turn Enhanced on while the global product default is off and confirm that the exact website becomes active in Enhanced mode.
6. Open all six product settings pages and the All Settings page. Refresh them quickly and switch among them. Confirm that the structure, cached control values, and en-US or zh-CN copy appear together without a blank or wrong-language frame. Confirm that All Settings remains the rightmost settings-page switch and that its six product cards open the correct pages.
7. From All Settings, choose Reset all settings and cancel once. Confirm that nothing changes. Confirm the next attempt and verify that product defaults, website rules, explicit language choice, active image and video sessions, and temporary toolbar state are reset while already-downloaded files remain available.
8. On a page with a wheel takeover handler, confirm that Native Scroll preserves browser scrolling and that the browser toolbar activity artwork can still change independently from the popup's blue state model.
9. Add the hostname to Native Scroll's Disabled sites and confirm that protection does not start there. Repeat with No Autoplay and confirm that video, audio, and Web Audio may autoplay. Add a more-specific Enabled rule and confirm that it takes priority. Repeat the same exception pattern with Enhanced and Standard rules.
10. On a page that automatically calls `video.play()`, confirm that No Autoplay stops the video. Click both native and custom playback controls and confirm that the first trusted click starts playback and updates the page control normally.
11. On a page that starts audible audio or Web Audio automatically, confirm that No Autoplay blocks it without showing a page prompt. Turn on **Allow audio autoplay on all sites** and confirm that audio elements and Web Audio may start everywhere while autoplaying video remains blocked. Turn it off, add a website under **Always allow audio autoplay**, and confirm that only matching websites receive the same audio permission.
12. Add a hostname to No Autoplay Enhanced sites and confirm that existing and later video or audio elements are removed.
13. On a page that disables selection or changes copied text, click the Any Copy product icon. Confirm that only its icon becomes blue with a background and the original selection reaches the clipboard without an added suffix.
14. Click Any Copy Enhanced while Any Copy is off and confirm that only the enhanced icon becomes blue with a background. Enable Any Copy as well, then turn either product off and confirm that the other remains active.
15. Confirm that Any Copy Enhanced produces a selectable static reading view, preserves useful text and images, and restores the original page without reloading. Confirm that it uses its own content-script bridge and activity state rather than changing Any Copy.
16. On a page containing regular, responsive, lazy-loaded, linked, CSS, SVG, and canvas images, open the popup and click Image Download. Confirm that the already-prepared workspace opens in Chrome’s Side Panel without replacing or leaving the source tab, remains bound to that tab, and uses a usable single-column layout at a narrow width. Repeat several times and confirm that no separate workspace tab appears after a transient Side Panel error. Confirm that related variants stay grouped and the largest valid original candidate is recommended without hiding alternatives.
17. Confirm that Image Download's complete Filters card, including search, opens collapsed by default. Expand it and filter results by text, format, layout, dimensions, and sorting. Confirm that the active-filter count includes search, that a candidate refresh preserves the expanded state and all values, and that clearing filters returns the count to zero. Select visible results and verify separate and ZIP downloads. Confirm that original format is preserved by default and that JPEG, PNG, and WebP conversion happens only after selection.
18. Use Image Download’s area capture, normal rescan, and deep scan. Confirm that the selected visible region becomes a result, the rescan icon is a single circular arrow, deep scan reveals compatible lazy images, and the source page returns to its original scroll position. During a scan, confirm that both scan controls remain unavailable and rapid clicks do not start another scan. Confirm that rapid clicks on open, capture, stop, or download controls also produce one action. Use the Side Panel action to open the full workspace in a separate tab, then change the workspace-location setting and confirm the next session opens directly in that tab. Change the setting back to Side Panel and confirm that an opening failure leaves the source tab selected instead of silently creating a separate tab.
19. On a page with a direct MP4 or WebM source, click Video Download. Confirm that its icon becomes blue without a background and its result view opens immediately. Confirm that the result header shows the Cosmic Gemini mark, the Video Download icon, and the compact product wordmark in that order. Verify that the selected file is streamed locally before being handed to Chrome Downloads. Return to the main popup while the session remains active, close the popup, and open it again. Confirm that it stays on the main view until Video Download is selected again.
20. On a page with a compatible HLS master playlist, confirm that available variants and alternate audio are shown, the selected playlist is assembled without loading the complete output into memory, and its temporary artifact is removed when Chrome finishes or interrupts the download.
21. With No Autoplay enabled, open `https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1yM4y1t7zB/` without starting playback. Confirm that Video Download shows the video thumbnail and title, presents one concise option for each main quality, and keeps an open quality menu stable while status polling continues. Start a selected format and use the red cancel icon while it is being prepared. Confirm that processing stops, no Chrome download begins, partial local files and media-header rules are removed, and the format can be selected again. Then complete a selected video after local audio-video merging and confirm that a CDN returning a complete response without Range support produces no Mediabunny warning. Confirm that Chrome uses the displayed video title rather than the temporary artifact identifier as its filename. Choose Audio only and confirm that the standalone track uses the same title when available. Repeat on YouTube and confirm that combined or locally remuxed formats and subtitles remain available.
22. On a compatible unprotected DASH page, confirm that representations are expanded and selected audio and video tracks produce one MP4 or MKV file. Confirm that a protected representation remains unavailable.
23. Confirm that Image Download and Video Download continue across same-origin navigation, clear old candidates, and stop after cross-origin navigation, source-tab closure, or their stop controls. Confirm that inactive tabs are not scanned.
24. Add wildcard activation and mode rules that cover the current website. Use the popup controls and confirm that they create exact-host overrides rather than deleting the broader wildcard. Remove the exact overrides and confirm that the broader rules apply again. Repeat with wildcard Any Copy and Any Copy Enhanced rules and confirm that their independent controls change only their own activation list.
25. Rapidly click each popup state control twice and confirm that only one action is accepted. In Settings, repeat with switches, selects, Add, and rule removal controls. Confirm that one save occurs and that a failed save restores the last confirmed value.
26. Navigate away and confirm that the toolbar intervention icon returns to its default state.
27. Open Satellites and enable Bili Daily Login while a Bilibili account is already signed in to Chrome. Confirm that an incomplete task is scheduled without opening or inspecting a Bilibili tab, that later work follows the 00:05 China Standard Time boundary, and that disabling the feature clears its alarm.
