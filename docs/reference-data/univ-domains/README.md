# univ-domains

`univ-domains` is the internal technical name of the academic, research and cultural institution email-domain reference owned by Mailto Capture. `registry.json` is its reviewed build input. It contains public institution facts, domain aliases, principal-campus locations and cohort membership. It never contains captured user email addresses. The reference identifies institutional domains, not individual mailbox addresses; shared government/service roots are not attributed to one school.

## Source coverage and remaining gaps

The mainland audit, `mainland-domain-audit.json`, accounts for every **3,196** entry in the 2026 MOE list (2,952 ordinary and 244 adult institutions). **2,093** are mapped; **865** currently have only outside-policy homepages in the reviewed sources; **216** have no usable published domain in those sources; **22** need shared-host identity review. Directory-derived mainland and Taiwan academic identities require an explicit `edu/ac/gov/mil` DNS class, optionally followed by a two-letter country suffix. Substrings such as `edu.example.com` do not qualify. Institutional identities and aliases require reviewed source evidence; this class restriction does not apply to Hong Kong, Macau or other regions. Two civil Taiwan institutions have only outside-policy hosts in the official list and are explicitly excluded in the source metadata. A missing domain is not proof that a school has no permitted domain; unresolved entries are deliberately not guessed. Domain facts from GlobalUniversityHomePages are joined by exact MOE school code; public directory supplements use exact Chinese names. Source spelling changes do not authorize fuzzy matching to another institution.

The public National Universities transcription has 427 entries, including unranked institutions, and was read to its empty final page. The public liberal-arts transcription has duplicate/missing entries at tied pagination boundaries. After disambiguating the two Wheaton colleges and the St. John’s campuses, 196 distinct campus entries are recoverable; official institution reports repair three more omissions. The resulting **199** entries include unranked institutions and are **not** represented as a complete 203-ranked-institution publisher export. The separate NCES arts/sciences cohort broadens domain coverage without inventing U.S. News membership. Known closed Birmingham–Southern, Wells and Magdalen are excluded from that supplement. Ranked/unranked status is never shown in Mailto Capture.

The complete U.S. News graduate publisher directory was not readable during preparation. Do not claim that the reference is a verified complete export of Best Graduate Schools. To reduce omissions of professional and specialized schools, the separate official NCES cohorts select active degree-granting four-year institutions in sectors 1/2/3 with graduate offerings or a Carnegie engineering/business/arts special focus. `us-institution-audit.json` accounts for all **2,102** selected source rows: **2,090** mapped, **9** awaiting shared-host review and **3** awaiting host review. The graduate cohort maps **2,065 of 2,077**; all **112** special-focus source rows are mapped. System offices are excluded from that degree-granting selection and curated separately, so system mail domains must not be inferred from one ranked campus. First-party Pratt/Rose-Hulman subject-ranking reports are separately labeled rather than used to imply complete publisher coverage.

`medical-school-audit.json` accounts for every **237** unique school ID in the public [Admit.org directory](https://med.admit.org/school-rankings). **236** map to existing university or reviewed independent academic identities. A school using a shared healthcare-service root is retained as a documented gap rather than assigning the entire provider domain to that school. University medical faculties on the same registrable domain retain their parent university identity; for example, `med.umich.edu` remains University of Michigan. Independently registered academic domains, including `mayo.edu`, are reviewed using official institution/medical-association sources. Source shorthand and admission statistics never become runtime names or data. Multiple source school/campus rows can map to one email-domain identity; email alone cannot distinguish them.

## Sources and identity decisions

Each cohort in `registry.json` records its edition, source URLs, expected membership count and, where snapshots are used, their SHA-256 hashes. System/administrative-office records additionally retain per-record official evidence links. QS and the regional education directories provide public first-party lists. Because U.S. News did not provide readable complete publisher tables during preparation, the U.S. News cohorts use the explicitly cited public Menggy, FindingSchool, College Kickstart and XuanXiao transcriptions, plus recorded official institution reports. Do not describe these transcriptions as direct publisher exports.

Domain alignment uses [Hipo's MIT-licensed directory](https://github.com/Hipo/university-domains-list), the [MIT-licensed GlobalUniversityHomePages dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/XUEHANGAI/GlobalUniversityHomePages), official institution links in the education directories, and reviewed institution aliases. The bundled license is at `extension/content/mailto-capture/univ-domains-LICENSE.txt`. Candidate fuzzy matches were not accepted automatically. The runtime performs no fuzzy matching, rank lookup or remote update. Ordinary institution hosts use eTLD+1, calculated during source curation with the project’s shared site-key rules. Only explicitly reviewed distinct campus/institution namespaces retain subdomains, recorded in source-only `subdomainIdentities`; redundant web/mail/department prefixes are removed rather than enumerated. The offline compiler rejects undeclared subdomain identities and redundant subhosts. It never promotes a shared academic/government/admissions portal into one college’s root identity. No PSL lookup is added to the webpage runtime.

U.S. states use the NCES IPEDS HD2024 directory with explicit principal-campus corrections for shared/system domains; the HD2025 ZIP was not publicly retrievable during preparation. Canadian provinces follow the Universities Canada directory and institution main-campus locations. The location describes the institution's legal principal campus, not the email user's location or a department's IP address. System and administrative-office entries instead describe the organization and the state of its official administrative headquarters. They never impersonate a constituent campus. More specific independently listed campus hosts still take precedence through the longest explicit host match. Do not infer a campus from an unrelated department label.

Some institutional domains have historical or student-only alternatives. Keep confirmed aliases attached to the same institution, but do not infer aliases from similar spelling. In particular, `cuhk.edu.cn` has its own Shenzhen record and China region; `hkust-gz.edu.cn` likewise identifies the Guangzhou university in China. Neither receives its Hong Kong partner's location. Generic suffixes and shared email providers are not school identifiers. A directory's prefixed Taiwan institutional web host (`w3`, `www1`, etc.) also retains the corresponding institution's `.edu.tw` root.

The `us-systems` cohort includes `cccco.edu`, `calstate.edu`, `ucop.edu`, `suny.edu`, `cuny.edu`, `utsystem.edu`, `tamus.edu`, `usg.edu`, `northcarolina.edu`, `wisconsin.edu` (plus its confirmed `uwsa.edu` email alias), `vccs.edu`, `nccommunitycolleges.edu`, `tcsg.edu`, `accs.edu`, `kctcs.edu`, `minnstate.edu`, `iu.edu`, `inter.edu`, `cu.edu`, `hawaii.edu`, `alaska.edu` and `upr.edu`. Official source URLs are recorded with each entry. Shared SUNY/CUNY roots are accepted only as explicitly evidenced system records; generic education suffixes remain prohibited. `cu.edu` identifies University of Colorado rather than Boulder; Boulder/Denver/Colorado Springs/Anschutz retain their own domains. Likewise, `hawaii.edu` identifies the system, while `hilo.hawaii.edu` and `manoa.hawaii.edu` retain their campus identities.

The Dearborn record retains both `umdearborn.edu` and `umd.umich.edu`, as documented in the university’s [domain policy](https://umdearborn.edu/policies-and-procedures/university-it-policies-standards/subdomain-policy-and-procedure-requesting) and [admissions directory](https://www.catalog.umd.umich.edu/undergraduate/admissions/). These explicit hosts and their subdomains identify University of Michigan–Dearborn before the broader `umich.edu` match. Other Michigan addresses retain University of Michigan.

The `affiliated-campuses` cohort explicitly covers Michigan–Flint; CUHK Shenzhen; HKUST Guangzhou; NYU Shanghai/Abu Dhabi; Nottingham Ningbo/Malaysia; Xi’an Jiaotong–Liverpool; Duke Kunshan; Monash Malaysia; Curtin Malaysia/Dubai; Swinburne Sarawak; Wollongong Dubai; Northwestern Qatar; Carnegie Mellon Qatar; Beijing Normal–Hong Kong Baptist University; and Xiamen University Malaysia. Every record has source links and covered-institution affiliation IDs. These relationships include joint universities and do not imply that every institution is owned by another. Campus location is its own physical country/region; U.S./Canadian campuses use their own state/province. A shared parent email domain alone cannot identify an individual campus, and the runtime never guesses from a mailbox’s local part.

Domain aliases remain attached to a single identity: `wustl.edu`/`washu.edu`, `swinburne.edu.au`/`swin.edu.au`, `shnyu.edu.cn`/`shanghai.nyu.edu`, and `dku.edu.cn`/`dukekunshan.edu.cn` have official evidence links in the records. `uic.edu.cn` deliberately retains the former United International College name, while `bnbu.edu.cn` uses the renamed university; the two display records are linked in source metadata rather than forced into one alias group. `hku.edu` is an explicit user-requested compatibility alias of `hku.hk`, recorded as such in `aliasNotes`; it is not represented as independently verified university ownership. Existing `ouhk.edu.hk`/`hkmu.edu.hk` and `hsmc.edu.hk`/`hsu.edu.hk` mappings remain intact. More specific campus hosts always outrank broader school aliases.

The `uncc.edu` legacy email domain and `charlotte.edu` identify the same University of North Carolina at Charlotte record. The university’s [Google account migration notice](https://oneit.charlotte.edu/2024/02/08/google-account-rename/) explicitly retains the old email alias; subdomains follow the existing DNS-label-boundary rules.

## Cultural institution selection

The `cultural-institutions` cohort is a curated selection of established museums, research libraries and cultural foundations with their own `.edu` or `.edu` country-class domains. Each record retains official evidence of its identity, cultural mission and nonprofit or public status. A public museum or library is eligible alongside private nonprofits; Smithsonian is recorded as a public trust, not a private charity. This is not an exhaustive worldwide directory, a domain-wide trust rule or mailbox authentication. The internal reference name remains `univ-domains`.

Shared roots identify their actual institution: `si.edu` identifies Smithsonian Institution and `getty.edu` identifies J. Paul Getty Trust, not an inferred individual museum or program. `artic.edu` remains distinct from the art school’s `saic.edu`; Folger retains its own library identity and Washington, DC location rather than inheriting Amherst College’s name and state. U.S. locations use institutional headquarters states, including District of Columbia. Existing regional name and bilingual display conventions also apply to cultural institutions. A planned future rebranding does not replace the name in use on the review date.

## Nonprofit research institution selection

The `nonprofit-research-institutions` cohort adds individually reviewed, established nonprofit scientific and scholarly research institutions, consortia and university-affiliated research centers with their own educational domains. Official mission, nonprofit-status and headquarters evidence stays in the registry; eligibility is never inferred from a `.edu` suffix. Museums such as Franklin Institute remain in the separate cultural cohort. No additional network requests, reputation scores or observed-email storage are introduced.

Retain institutional boundaries even when collaborations, geography or names overlap: Institute for Advanced Study is not Princeton University; Marine Biological Laboratory is not an alias of University of Chicago or neighboring Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution; Johns Hopkins APL keeps its distinct laboratory domain. Scripps Research is distinct from Scripps College and the San Diego oceanography unit. Carnegie Institution for Science is distinct from Carnegie Mellon University. Scientific consortia retain their own identity rather than one member university’s name. Locations describe the institution headquarters, not the sender’s physical location.

## Display policy

- English display names omit a leading `The University of` in every case, including supplemental English rows. Other non-distinctive leading articles are omitted after review; distinctive names such as `The Ohio State University` and `The New School` retain theirs. Do not remove internal articles, such as `University of the Pacific`, or rewrite source-membership evidence. This is display style rather than a claim that the official legal name has changed.
- All school display names, including optional English rows, use `and` instead of `&`, with spaces around the word even in initials. Original source-member spellings remain unchanged. Use the full `Oregon Health and Science University`, never directory shorthand such as `Oregon University`.
- Review institution names individually; do not title-case source strings or replace every comma with a campus separator. The SUNY university centers use the full names in the [authorized campus-name table](https://www.suny.edu/media/suny/content-assets/documents/CampusNames_December-2022.pdf) and the [current research directory](https://www.suny.edu/impact/research/). Geneseo/Purchase/Old Westbury likewise use reviewed full institutional names. The CUNY system displays `City University of New York`; Hunter College and Medgar Evers College retain their individual identities. Per-record `nameEvidence` links record the name review without entering the bundled data. Preserve original cohort spellings as source evidence, not as the display name.
- Use `University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill` and the reviewed lowercase connecting words in other English names. Preserve proper internal capitalization such as DePaul/DeSales/D’Youville. `University of Illinois at Chicago` is an explicitly requested display form; [UIC’s current style guide](https://brand.uic.edu/messaging/editorial-and-style-guide/) instead uses `University of Illinois Chicago`, and this distinction is retained in the source notes. `University of California, San Diego` deliberately keeps its comma. Missing spaces, duplicated spaces and source-wide title casing are not valid reasons to overwrite these reviewed forms.
- Mainland/Hong Kong/Macau/Taiwan institutions use Simplified Chinese primary school names. In English UI, mainland English-name rows are limited to the reviewed 985/211/Double First-Class identities and their explicitly linked affiliated campuses. Undergraduate status or a world-ranking entry alone does not qualify. HK/MO/TW include an English-name row only when a reliable English name is available; missing names never create an empty row. Mainland-origin overseas campuses with distinct reviewed hosts (such as Xiamen University Malaysia) also use Chinese primary names plus English names in English UI, while keeping their own physical country/region. Missing reliable English names produce no empty row. Chinese UI omits all supplemental English-name rows. Other institutions use Latin-character names without parenthesized common acronyms. Legal foundation wrappers are omitted from Taiwan display names; original names remain in membership evidence.
- Use an en dash (`–`) for campus separators, such as University of Michigan–Dearborn and University of Wisconsin–Madison. Standalone dash separators also use an en dash; preserve real compound-word/place-name hyphens such as Aix-Marseille and Urbana-Champaign. Original source membership spellings remain unchanged as evidence.
- School names and country/region occupy separate lines beneath each original To/CC/BCC address. Unknown addresses have no empty metadata rows or error message. School metadata never enters copied addresses/messages.
- U.S. and Canadian institutions include the full principal-campus state/province. U.S. country text is always `USA`.
- English regional labels: `China`, `Hong Kong, China`, `Macau, China`, `Chinese Taipei`. Simplified Chinese labels: `中国`, `中国香港`, `中国澳门`, `中华台北`. Other country names follow the interface language.

## Regeneration

After reviewing changes to `registry.json`, run:

```sh
node scripts/update-univ-domains.mjs
node scripts/update-univ-domains.mjs --check
node --test test/mailto-capture-runtime.test.mjs
```

The generator is intentionally offline and deterministic. It rejects missing cohort members, duplicate institution/domain identities, broad suffixes, missing North American locations, and missing or inappropriate display names. Mainland, U.S. graduate/special-focus, medical and regional source audits are validated against exact cohort membership. Only eligible bilingual rows carry an additional English name; eligibility is compiled from reviewed identities and affiliation relationships, never inferred at runtime. The compiled reference is **423,159 bytes** (about 423 KB), with a 500 KB build guard. The compiled file omits membership/rank evidence, affiliation graphs and unused translations; its lookup maps are built only when an email is first inspected. Neither settings nor the runtime stores observed addresses. Preparation scratch files may be kept in the ignored `test-dist/univ-domains` directory; they are not shipped. New bulk directory reads retain only institution names, domains, category and location facts, not raw contact pages or complete mailbox addresses.

For regional maintenance, prioritize the official directory's institution identifier, current name and official website over secondary domain-directory names. The MQA data contains historical campus/program records; private colleges without reviewed bachelor-or-higher courses are outside that selection. SSG secondary-school diplomas do not qualify as tertiary courses. An OfS provider without direct degree powers or qualifying evidence remains a scope-review gap, not a definitive exclusion. Closed Academyinfo entries are excluded. Thai source rows come from an older official publication and must not be presented as a verified current ministry export. The Mongolian ministry list and APNNIC names are complementary sources, not interchangeable membership lists. Do not assign a shared government/ministry root, a hosting provider or a path-only foreign campus to an individual school. Reviewed Malaysian institution namespaces and the Aizu junior-college namespace remain explicit subdomain identities. Canadian cross-province source rows use the institution's principal province, never whichever branch happens to appear first.

The `maine.edu` root identifies University of Maine System; `usm.maine.edu` separately identifies University of Southern Maine. The UC Office of the President and South Carolina System retain their own administrative identities and headquarters states. Source cohort membership does not authorize replacing a reviewed campus name with the registrant's abbreviation or labeling a shared parent root as a regional branch.

The shared `csbsju.edu` host identifies College of Saint Benedict and Saint John’s University together; it does not arbitrarily select one college. The explicitly reviewed `morris.umn.edu` host identifies University of Minnesota–Morris before the broader Minnesota root. Duplicate Shanghai Jiao Tong/Minnesota web-host records are consolidated into their existing institutional identity without dropping source membership.

## Regional completeness and shared-host limits

The Taiwan civil list is no longer filtered to general universities. All 149 official rows are reviewed: 66 general universities, 73 technological/vocational institutions, 8 religious institutes and 2 open universities. Two religious institutes have no permitted host in that source, leaving 147 bundled civil institutions. The official military-academy directory adds seven higher-education schools (not the secondary preparatory school); the two police higher-education schools are also included. Keep `afats.khc.edu.tw` specific; never claim the broader `khc.edu.tw` education host. Official directory English names are reliable; military/police entries without a reviewed current English name omit the optional row. Do not import presidents’ names, their email addresses or phone numbers from the civil source.

HKU SPACE and its Community College share a descriptive group. The Macau source directory contains ten institutions; nine have bundled institutional domains. Indiana and Inter American system roots are separate from the explicit `southeast.iu.edu` and `metro.inter.edu` campus hosts. Preserve true name hyphens (Hampden-Sydney, Randolph-Macon, Gardner-Webb, Bethune-Cookman, Harris-Stowe and East-West); campus separators use en dashes.
